A proper local, down by the water

Callum Reece opened Harbour & Hone in 2016, after fifteen years behind the chair. He wanted a shop that felt like the ones he grew up with: somewhere you could get a sharp cut without any fuss, and where the conversation was as good as the coffee.

The brick is exposed, the chairs are leather, and the radio is usually on a soul record. Regulars pop in for a chat. If it's your first time, you'll feel just as welcome.
